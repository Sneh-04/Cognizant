Community Bulletin

Monthly Newsletter — June 2026

The city council has announced a new green initiative that aims to plant over 5,000 trees across all wards by the end of the year. Residents are encouraged to participate in the planting drives scheduled every weekend.

Road repairs on Main Street will begin July 1st and are expected to last three weeks. Commuters are advised to use alternate routes via South Boulevard during this period.

The public library will now remain open until 9 PM on weekdays, following a request from residents for extended hours. New digital kiosks have also been installed for faster catalog browsing.

Local schools will participate in the annual Science Olympiad on August 10. Registration for student teams is open at the Education Department office or online at the city portal.
